Our Story

Sophisticated financial strategies should not be the exclusive domain of the largest institutions.

Aevum Capital was founded on a straightforward premise: sophisticated financial strategies should not be the exclusive domain of the largest institutions. Individuals and families with meaningful wealth deserve access to the same risk management tools, alternative asset classes, and structured solutions that institutional investors have long relied upon.

The firm was built around two complementary disciplines — permanent life insurance as a foundational wealth protection and accumulation vehicle, and life settlement funds as a non-correlated alternative investment. Together, these strategies address the core concerns of our clients: protecting principal, preserving liquidity, and generating predictable, risk-adjusted returns.

We work with a select group of clients who value discretion, depth of analysis, and a long-term perspective. Our engagements are educational first — we believe informed clients make better decisions, and better decisions lead to better outcomes.

Our Philosophy

Three principles that guide how we think about wealth and risk.

Every recommendation we make is grounded in three principles that guide how we think about wealth and risk.

01

Protect Principal First

Capital preservation is the foundation of long-term wealth. Before pursuing returns, we ensure that the strategies we recommend have defined downside parameters and structural protections built in.

02

Preserve Liquidity

Illiquidity is a risk that is frequently underpriced. We structure allocations to ensure clients maintain appropriate access to capital across different time horizons and market conditions.

03

Deliver Predictable Returns

We favor strategies with actuarially or contractually defined return profiles over speculative approaches. Predictability enables better planning, better tax management, and better sleep.

How We Work

Deliberate. Educational. Built around you.

Our process is deliberate, educational, and built around your specific situation.

01

Discovery

We begin with a comprehensive conversation about your financial picture, goals, risk tolerance, and time horizon. No assumptions, no generic recommendations.

02

Analysis

We model scenarios, stress-test assumptions, and evaluate how each strategy performs across a range of outcomes — not just the optimistic case.

03

Education

Before any recommendation is made, we ensure you understand the mechanics, risks, costs, and alternatives. An informed decision is always the right decision.

04

Implementation

We coordinate with carriers, fund managers, legal counsel, and tax advisors to ensure seamless execution aligned with your broader financial plan.

05

Ongoing Review

Financial strategies require monitoring. We conduct regular reviews to ensure your allocations remain aligned with your evolving goals and market conditions.

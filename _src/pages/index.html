01

What Is a Life Settlement?

A life settlement occurs when a life insurance policyholder sells their existing policy to a third-party investor for a lump sum greater than the cash surrender value but less than the death benefit. The buyer assumes premium payments and becomes the beneficiary.

02

The Secondary Market Arbitrage

Policies are frequently surrendered or lapsed at a fraction of their intrinsic value. Aevum Capital identifies and acquires these undervalued policies, capturing the spread between the discounted purchase price and the contractual death benefit — a structural inefficiency in the market.

03

Why It Generates Predictable Returns

Unlike equities or real estate, life settlement returns are driven by actuarial science — not market sentiment. The death benefit is a contractual obligation of the issuing insurance carrier, making the ultimate payout both defined and predictable within a statistical framework.

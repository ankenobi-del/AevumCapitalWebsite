Why Life Insurance Belongs in Every Financial Plan

At its core, life insurance is a promise — a contractual guarantee that the people and institutions you care about will be financially protected regardless of what happens to you. Yet for many individuals and families, the right policy structure remains elusive.

Aevum Capital approaches life insurance not as a commodity product, but as a precision instrument. We analyze your income replacement needs, estate tax exposure, business continuity requirements, and legacy objectives to design a solution that fits your life — not a generic template.

Permanent Life Insurance

Lifelong Coverage with Lasting Value

Permanent life insurance provides coverage that never expires, paired with a cash value component that grows on a tax-advantaged basis over time. It is the cornerstone of estate planning, wealth transfer, and long-term financial security.

Most predictable

Whole Life

Guaranteed death benefit, fixed premiums, and a cash value that grows at a contractually guaranteed rate. The most predictable and stable form of permanent coverage.

Most flexible

Universal Life

Flexible premium structure with adjustable death benefit. Allows policyholders to adapt coverage as income and financial needs evolve over time.

Upside + floor

Indexed Universal Life (IUL)

Cash value growth linked to a market index (such as the S&P 500) with a floor that protects against market losses. Combines upside participation with downside protection.

Growth potential

Variable Universal Life (VUL)

Investment-linked cash value with the potential for higher growth. Suitable for sophisticated policyholders comfortable with market-linked performance.

  • 01Estate planning and wealth transfer to heirs
  • 02Funding buy-sell agreements between business partners

Term Life Insurance

Targeted Protection When It Matters Most

Term life insurance delivers the highest death benefit per premium dollar for a defined period — typically 10, 20, or 30 years. It is the most cost-effective way to protect against income loss during your highest-earning, highest-obligation years.

Level Term

Fixed premiums and a consistent death benefit for the full policy term. Ideal for income replacement, mortgage protection, and family security during working years.

Return of Premium

A term policy that refunds all premiums paid if you outlive the coverage period — combining protection with a built-in savings component.

Convertible Term

Provides the option to convert to a permanent policy without new underwriting, offering flexibility as your financial situation evolves.

Common Applications

  • 01Income replacement for young families and dependents
  • 02Mortgage and debt protection during repayment periods
  • 03Business loan collateral and SBA requirements
  • 04Temporary coverage during estate planning transitions
  • 05Affordable protection while building long-term assets
